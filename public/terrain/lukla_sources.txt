This is a surface model, including vegetation and buildings, not a surveyed bare-earth airport model. Source spacing is nominally 30 m; the bundled 22 km grid has 42.97 m sample spacing and the main render mesh about 86 m. Airport facilities, runway heights and local grading remain simulator approximations. No artificial regional cliff or departure canyon is added. See lukla_metadata.json for projection, source URL and checksums.
